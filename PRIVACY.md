# Privacy Policy — Weather Alarm PH

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026

Weather Alarm PH ("the app") gives weather forecasts, alarms with weather advice, and local class and work suspension announcements for users in the Philippines. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and who it is shared with.

**In short:** the app has no user accounts, and the developer does not run servers that collect your personal data. Your settings stay on your phone. Weather services receive your approximate location in order to return a forecast. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects data as described in Google's policies.

## 1. Information the app uses

### Approximate location
If you allow it, the app reads your phone's **approximate (city-level) location**. It uses this to:
- choose the nearest covered city (for example, City of Isabela or Zamboanga City) for PAGASA information and suspension announcements, and
- request a forecast for where you are.

Your location is kept **only on your phone**. To get a forecast, the app sends the location's coordinates to the weather services listed in section 2. You can stop sharing your location at any time in your phone's Settings, and choose a city by hand inside the app.

### Information stored only on your phone
- Your alarm times and whether they are on
- Your language choice (English or Filipino)
- Your chosen city or "use my location" setting
- Recently downloaded forecasts, saved so that alarms still work without internet
- Whether Premium is active

This information is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

### Voice readout (Premium)
The forecast is read aloud using your phone's text-to-speech engine. The text is created on your phone.

## 2. Services the app connects to

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) | Coordinates, internet (IP) address | Forecast data (including ECMWF and GFS models) |
| MET Norway (api.met.no) | Coordinates, internet (IP) address | Forecast data |
| GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) | Internet (IP) address | Downloads the daily DOST-PAGASA information and suspension announcements published for the app |
| Google AdMob (free version only) | Device and ad identifiers, IP address, app usage and ad interactions | Showing and measuring ads |
| Google Play | Your Google account and purchase details | Processing Premium subscriptions |

Like any website, these services may record your IP address when the app connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply:
- Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
- MET Norway: https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService
- GitHub: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- Google (AdMob and Play): https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 3. Advertising (free version)
The free version shows a banner ad at the bottom of the main screen, served by **Google AdMob**. Google may use your device's advertising ID and other information to show and measure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (**Settings → Google → Ads**, or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, depending on the phone). Premium removes ads. Ads never appear on the alarm screen.

## 4. Payments (Premium)
Premium subscriptions (₱49 per month or ₱500 per year) are handled entirely by **Google Play**. The developer never sees your card, GCash, or other payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the Google Play Store under **Payments & subscriptions**.

## 5. Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Approximate location | Pick your city and get your local forecast |
| Notifications | Ring alarms and show weather alerts |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | Ring alarms at the exact time you set |
| Full-screen notifications | Show the alarm card over the lock screen |
| Run at startup | Restore your alarms after the phone restarts |
| Internet | Download forecasts, PAGASA information and announcements |

## 6. Accuracy of information
Forecasts combine DOST-PAGASA information with global weather models. Class and work suspension announcements are copied from official LGU announcements and link to their source. Always follow official announcements from DOST-PAGASA, your LGU, DepEd and your school or employer. The app is a convenience, not an official warning service.

## 7. Children
The app is meant for a general audience, including students. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Users under 18 should use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 8. Your rights
Under the Philippine **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, you have the right to be informed about, to access, to correct, and to object to the processing of your personal data. Because the developer does not collect or store your personal data, most requests can be met by changing your phone's settings or uninstalling the app. For requests about data held by the services in section 2, please contact them directly.
